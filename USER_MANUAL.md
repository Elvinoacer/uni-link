# 📘 Uni Social App - User Manual

## 1. Introduction & Purpose
**Uni Social** is a dedicated social platform for university students. It bridges the gap between campus life, social networking, and practical student needs.
**Core Purpose**: To provide a safe, verified exclusive space for students to connect, trade, and discover events.

## 2. Getting Started
### Onboarding
1.  **Select University**: Choose your campus from the supported list.
2.  **Verification**: Enter your `.edu` email to verify your student status. Only verified students can access the platform.
3.  **Profile Setup**: Create a unique `@handle`, add a display name, and upload an avatar.

## 3. Navigation (The Tab Bar)
The app is organized into 5 main tabs at the bottom of the screen:

### 🏠 **Home** (Feed)
- **Global Tab**: See posts from everyone on your campus.
- **Following Tab**: See posts only from people you follow.
- **Identity toggle**: Tap the "Post as..." switcher to toggle between **Real Name** (Public) and **Alias** (Anonymous/Masked) mode.
- **Interactions**: Like, Repost, and Reply to threads.

### 🔍 **Search**
- **People**: Find classmates and friends.
- **Content**: Search for specific topics or hashtags in posts.
- **Trending**: See what hashtags are currently viral on campus.

### 🛍️ **Marketplace** (Student-to-Student)
- **Browse**: Find textbooks, dorm supplies, and electronics.
- **Filters**: Sort by category (Textbooks, Electronics, etc.) or distance.
- **Sell**: Tap the blue banner to list an item. 
  - *Smart Feature*: Use **ISBN Lookup** to auto-fill textbook details instantly.
- **Safety**: Listings show "Verified Student" badges.

### 📅 **Events**
- **Discovery**: Toggle between **"Near Me"** (Geofenced) and **"All Events"**.
- **RSVP**: Tap "RSVP Now" to save an event to your calendar.
- **Map View**: See where events are happening on campus (Visual only).

### 👤 **Profile**
- **My Activity**: View your own posts and replies.
- **Edit Profile**: Update your bio or avatar.
- **Settings**: Manage notifications, blocked users, and privacy.

## 4. Key Features & Expectations

### 🎭 Anonymity (The "Mask" Mode)
- You can post anonymously! When posting, toggle to **Alias** mode.
- Anonymous posts have a distinct yellow/fox icon.
- **Expectation**: Anonymous posts are still moderated. Hate speech or harassment will result in a ban, even if posted anonymously.

### 💬 Messaging
- **DMs**: Direct Message other students for social chat or marketplace negotiations.
- **Real-time**: Messages appear instantly.

### 🛡️ Moderation & Safety
- **Reporting**: If you see inappropriate content, tap the **"..."** menu on a post and select **Report**.
- **Context**: You can specify if it's spam, harassment, or misinformation.
- **Blocking**: You can block users to hide their content from your feed.

## 5. Troubleshooting
- **"Network Error"**: Ensure you are connected to campus Wi-Fi or valid data.
- **"Location Services"**: Grant location permissions to see "Nearby" events.
- **Notifications**: Check your system settings if you aren't receiving push alerts for DMs.
