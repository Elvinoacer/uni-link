## 2. Description & Details Section
### Detailed Instructions
1. **Download & Join**: Install the Kampus app from the given link and sign up using your student email or phone number.
2. **Select Your Campus**: Search for your university to join your local community. No institutional login is required to get started.
3. **Set Up Your Profile**: Personalize your profile with a display name and bio. Choose whether you want to post as your verified self or a pseudonym relative to your student community.
4. **Explore the Feed**: Start following your peers, join discussions, and vote on campus polls to stay in the loop.
5. **Trade in the Marketplace**: To sell an item, simply snap a photo, add a price, and post it to the marketplace. For textbooks, use the ISBN scanner to fetch details automatically.
6. **Report or Reclaim Lost Items**: If you found something, post it with a "Verification Question" (e.g., "What's the sticker on the back?"). This ensures the item only goes back to its rightful owner.
7. **Discover Events**: Check the Events tab for upcoming campus rallies, study groups, or parties. Get real-time notifications for events happening right near you.

### Key Features
- **Hyperlocal Social Feed**: Engage with your campus community through short-form posts, polls, and rich media updates.
- **Campus Marketplace**: A trusted peer-to-peer marketplace for textbooks, electronics, and dorm essentials with ISBN integration.
- **Lost & Found System**: Reclaim lost items safely using a private verification question system that prevents fraudulent claims.
- **Event Discovery & Management**: Discover student-led events with interactive maps, RSVPs, and geofenced push notifications.
- **Identity Shielding**: Maintain privacy with the ability to toggle between verified and pseudonymous identities for honest campus discourse.
- **Real-time Messaging**: Secure, end-to-end encrypted direct messaging for campus-wide collaboration and marketplace coordination.

### Technical Architecture
Kampus is built on a modern **Microservices-based Monorepo** architecture designed for scalability and fault isolation:
- **Frontend**: **React Native (Expo)** with **NativeWind** for a premium, cross-platform mobile experience.
- **API Gateway**: A lightweight **Express.js** proxy for secure request routing and rate limiting.
- **Core Services**: **Node.js/TypeScript** microservices handling business logic, user management, and social features.
- **Database Layer**: **PostgreSQL** managed via **Prisma ORM**, with **Redis** for sub-second feed caching and session management.
- **Real-time**: **Socket.io** integration for instant messaging and live notifications.
- **Media Infrastructure**: **Cloudinary** for image/video streaming and **Vercel Blob** for document storage.
